# Contact

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/basil/bible/issues) to report a mistake, ask an editorial question, or suggest an improvement.

For a textual issue, include:

- The book, chapter, and verse, using this edition’s numbering
- The words at issue, and whether you were reading the website or PDF
- The source you compared, with a link or page reference where possible
- The change you propose and why

For a website problem, include the page URL and what you expected to happen. There is no mailing list or store; GitHub issues are the contact channel for this project.
