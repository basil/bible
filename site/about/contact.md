# Contact

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/basil/bible/issues) to report an issue, ask an editorial question, or suggest an improvement.

For a textual issue, include:

- The book, chapter, and verse, using this edition’s numbering
- The words in question, and whether you were reading the website or PDF
- Any relevant source(s), with links or page references where possible
- The change you are proposing and the reason why you are proposing it

For a website problem, include the page URL and what you expected to happen.
