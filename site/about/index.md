# Orthodox Liturgical English Bible (OLEB)

The Orthodox Liturgical English Bible joins Brenton’s English Septuagint, with selected readings from Codex Alexandrinus, to the King James New Testament conformed to Robinson and Pierpont’s Byzantine text (2026). It includes the deuterocanonical books and revises spelling, vocabulary, grammar, and punctuation while retaining traditional English. The edition is available as a single-volume PDF and an online reader; [the introduction](/read/introduction) explains its texts, editorial choices, and conventions.

The reader includes the book’s front matter and notes, together with the website’s [tables of chapters and verses](/read/numbering) and [New Testament textual variants](/variants/).
