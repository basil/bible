# Textual Variants

Every verse where the Byzantine Greek differs from the Received Text, or where this edition corrects the King James English, book by book. In each entry the source text stands on the left and the adopted text on the right, the words that differ marked in colour; an ellipsis stands for words in agreement left out, and ⌀ for a place where a text has nothing. The [introduction](/variants/introduction) explains the texts compared.
