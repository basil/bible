# Verification

`make validate` verifies archive SHA-256 hashes, each original USFM member's hash, every chapter and verse label, and all marker counts. It enforces the selection of all 52 Brenton and 27 KJV scripture sources, the Orthodox order, Ezra-Nehemiah split, Daniel section grouping, Malachias 4 labels, Maccabees IV appendix, and the position of Brenton's Introduction to the Apocrypha. It compares transformed passages, notes, and styling against their pinned source passages. The front-matter order requires the KJV dedication immediately after Brenton's introduction, followed by the translators' preface. Rendered-text witnesses check the dedication's opening and ending, the translators' preface, the Acts closing line, and all 14 traditional epistle subscriptions. All original USFM files, including excluded files, are checked against their locked inventories.

Preparation checks that peripheral ID remapping leaves chapter/verse inventories and all source marker counts intact, and that the quotation tags and note spacer leave the printable content unchanged. It records quotation tags, the reversible pipe escape, and the nonprinting spacer needed for the reference-only note. After PTXprint preprocessing, each unit is compared against the generated project for exact printable content (ignoring only markup and whitespace), exact chapter/verse inventories, and counts of notes, references, italics, tables, and printed verse markers. The generated order is recorded explicitly. Source originals and processed project files are retained for comparison.

PDF verification uses qpdf, pdfinfo, pdffonts, and pdftotext. It checks every page's A5 dimensions, font embedding, expected quotation fonts, inherited publication text, and missing-glyph/TeX failures. The contents must converge between TeX runs, list every selected unit exactly once in manifest order, display the correct page numbers in the PDF, and point to pages containing the actual unit headings. `make check` builds twice with separate clean project and home/cache directories, checks matching text and page counts, and renders every page at 72 dpi for matching raster SHA-256 hashes. PDF metadata bytes need not match. Repeatability checks do not replace human proofreading.

Visual inspection must cover dense footnotes and their callers, Greek and RTL Hebrew quotations, poetic indentation, the long Jeremiah table title and table rows, contents, Ezra-Nehemiah and Malachias chapter boundaries, the Daniel sections, and the transitions between the Old Testament, its appendix, the New Testament, and historical apparatus. See [the visual inspection record](inspection.md) for page references and findings.

During implementation, isolated fault-injection checks confirmed that validation rejects a wrong archive checksum and that the processed-content comparison rejects a deleted footnote. `build/fault-injection.json` records the resulting errors.
