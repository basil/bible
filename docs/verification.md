# Verification

`make check-protrusion` compares the plain-XeTeX protrusion customization against stock XeLaTeX microtype for every present glyph in the edition's font families, faces, and representative sizes. It is also run by `make check`. Wording witnesses use PDF content-stream order because geometric text extraction can interleave the two columns when characters protrude into the gutter. Witness matching also checks a copy with numbered running headers removed, so page breaks cannot insert page furniture into a phrase; the separate layout extraction is retained for page and contents checks.

`make validate` verifies archive SHA-256 hashes, each original USFM member's hash, every chapter and verse label, and all marker counts. It enforces the selection of all 52 Brenton and 27 KJV scripture sources, the Orthodox order, Ezra-Nehemias split, Daniel section grouping, Malachias 4 labels, and the positions of the section dividers, each testament's front matter, and the appendices. It compares transformed passages, notes, and styling against their pinned source passages. It checks the SHA-256 hash of the marginal-notes transcription and parses exactly 775 New Testament notes. Each note must anchor to exactly one place in its verse, and each configured anchor and correction must be used. Removing the inserted notes must give back the unchanged Cambridge text, and each note's `fr` reference must match the verse that holds it. The front-matter order requires Brenton's preface, introduction, introduction to the Apocrypha, and abbreviations between the Old Testament divider and Genesis, and the KJV dedication and translators' preface between the New Testament divider and Matthew. Rendered-text witnesses check the dedication's opening and ending, the translators' preface, the Acts closing line, and all 14 traditional epistle subscriptions. All original USFM files, including excluded files, are checked against their locked inventories.

Preparation checks that peripheral ID remapping leaves chapter/verse inventories and all source marker counts intact, and that the quotation tags and note spacer leave the printable content unchanged. It verifies that every `toc1` long title matches the edition manifest while `toc2` and `h` use the configured short names and the `mt1`-`mt3` heading lines, joined in order, spell the long title, with Brenton's books laid out as the manifest's `heading` directs; expands `S.` to `Saint`; and permits no periods in `h`, `toc1`, or `mt1`-`mt3`. It records quotation tags, the reversible pipe escape, and the nonprinting spacer needed for the reference-only note. After PTXprint preprocessing, each unit is compared against the generated project for exact printable content (ignoring only markup and whitespace), exact chapter/verse inventories, and counts of notes, references, italics, tables, and printed verse markers. The generated order is recorded explicitly. Source originals and processed project files are retained for comparison.

PDF verification uses qpdf, pdfinfo, pdffonts, pdftohtml, and pdftotext. It checks every page's A5 dimensions, font embedding, expected text, verse-number, and quotation fonts, that the added words in Malachias 4:2 share the font of the roman words around them, inherited publication text, and missing-glyph/TeX failures. The contents must converge between TeX runs, list every selected unit exactly once in manifest order, display the correct page numbers in the PDF, and point to pages containing the actual unit headings. `make check` builds twice with separate clean project and home/cache directories, checks matching text and page counts, and renders every page at 72 dpi for matching raster SHA-256 hashes. PDF metadata bytes need not match. Repeatability checks do not replace human proofreading.

Visual inspection must cover dense footnotes and their callers, the New Testament marginal notes (callers before the glossed words, and the restored Greek in Acts 13:18 and 13:34), Greek and RTL Hebrew quotations, poetic indentation, the long Jeremias table title and table rows, contents, Ezra-Nehemias and Malachias chapter boundaries, the Daniel sections, and the transitions between the Old Testament, the New Testament, and the appendices, including each testament's front matter. See [the visual inspection record](inspection.md) for page references and findings.

During implementation, isolated fault-injection checks confirmed that validation rejects a wrong archive checksum, a changed marginal-notes checksum, a missing, wrong, or ambiguous note anchor, an unused correction, and a transcriber's remark left in a note, and that the processed-content comparison rejects a deleted footnote.
