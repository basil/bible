The superior lowercase letters a-z and the superior colon, which Valkyrie's
sups feature lacks, come from npxsups, the superiors font of Michael Sharpe's
newpx package (CTAN), scaled and raised to Valkyrie's own superior figures and
added as the glyphs sups selects for those characters, unhinted. npxsups is

The four faces are assembled by scripts/build_olebfont.py from the faces
make bootstrap finds on the host and the inputs pinned in Dockerfile. This
font notice does not govern the Bible text.
