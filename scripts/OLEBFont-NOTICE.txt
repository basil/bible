OLEBFont: Utopia with normalized Erewhon and Erewhon Math additions for the Orthodox Liturgical English Bible.

Utopia outlines, spacing, hinting and original OpenType typography are retained.
Erewhon supplies missing characters, alternate figures and small capitals.
Erewhon Math supplies glyphs absent from both text sources. Donor geometry,
spacing, positioning and hint dimensions are enlarged by 100/94 to restore
Utopia size. Donor local subroutines are expanded before scaling; hints remain.
The sole replacement of existing Utopia glyphs is superscript 1, 2 and 3,
which use Erewhon designs for consistency. No outlines are optically redesigned. Erewhon Italic’s tabular oldstyle zero
advance is corrected from 498 to 500 units to match the other nine figures. The four faces are assembled
by scripts/build_olebfont.py from the inputs pinned in Dockerfile.
